Privacy Policy

Effective August 13, 2026

PourAware respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the PourAware application for iOS and iPadOS.

Information We Collect

PourAware does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information.

Information you enter into PourAware, such as drink records, preferences, goals, and other app data, is stored locally on your device. PourAware does not send this information to the developer or to developer-operated servers.

Analytics and Tracking

PourAware does not use:

  • Advertising trackers
  • Third-party advertising services
  • Analytics services
  • Cross-app tracking
  • User profiling

PourAware does not track your activity across other apps or websites.

Third-Party Services

PourAware does not currently use third-party services that collect personal information from users.

Data Storage

Your PourAware data is stored locally on your device. The developer does not maintain a server-side database containing your drink history or other app data.

Your device's operating system or backup services may handle locally stored application data according to your device and iCloud settings. These services are provided and controlled by Apple, not PourAware.

Data Sharing

PourAware does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Children's Privacy

PourAware is intended as a personal awareness and moderation tool. It is not intended to encourage or promote alcohol consumption by minors.

PourAware does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if PourAware's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
